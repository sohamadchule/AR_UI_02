AKRUTI AR - FURNISH A ROOM AT REAL SIZE
Change log - 2026-10-26
=======================================

WHAT THE CUSTOMER CAN NOW DO (Android Chrome)
---------------------------------------------
1. Open AR from a product page. The floor is found and fixed on the first tap.
2. Place the product at its real catalogue size, standing exactly on the floor.
3. Add more products without leaving AR (+ Add furniture). All of them stay in
   the same room.
4. Tap any piece to select it, then:
     - drag it with one finger to move it along the floor
     - twist two fingers (or tap the 15 deg buttons) to turn it
     - change its colour, Move it to another spot, Duplicate it or Delete it
5. See warnings while arranging:
     - red outline: two pieces may overlap
     - amber outline: the gap is too narrow to walk through
     - the gap in cm to the nearest piece (and to the nearest wall, once the
       room is known)
6. Tell the app the room (Room & measure):
     - tap each corner of the room on the floor, or
     - type the room's width x depth, then mark one corner and one wall
   The app shows the room size, wall lengths and area, and flags any piece
   that goes past a wall.
7. Use a ruler: measure between any two points on the floor.
8. "Will it fit?" on the product page: enter the available width / depth /
   max height and get a plain answer ("20 cm too wide", "fits with 8 cm to
   spare", "fits only if turned sideways"). That space can also be placed in
   AR as a blue box (+ Add furniture -> Your space).

Products are never resized. If a product's size is not listed (or its 3D
model doesn't match the listed size) it is shown at the model's own size with
a notice, and nothing is blocked.


PHASE 1 - REAL SIZE
-------------------
Before: AR drew every GLB at the file's own scale and ignored the catalogue
size. Seed models came out about half size (sofa) or kilometres wide (chair,
desk, tables, lamp). Pinch-to-resize let the size be changed.

Now (src/lib/trueScale.ts, used by the AR engine):
  - The loaded model is measured vertex by vertex and compared with the
    catalogue size. If all three sides need the same factor (within 5%), the
    model is scaled by it. This fixes files exported in cm, mm or odd units.
  - If that only works with width and depth swapped, the model was exported
    side-on and is turned 90 degrees.
  - If the shape doesn't match: drawn as close to the listed size as its
    shape allows, with a notice giving the listed size and how far off it is.
    (Originally it was drawn at the file's own size. That put 687 km desks in
    the room; fixed after the phone test.)
  - No size listed: the file's size, corrected by a power of ten if its units
    are clearly wrong, with a notice.
  - Anything still bigger than 6 m or smaller than 5 cm is refused with a
    message.
  - Pinch-to-resize removed.

Seed results:
  Mahal sofa   real size  212 x 91 x 75 cm   (listed 212 x 88 x 76)
  Ira chair    real size  78 x 81 x 97 cm    (listed 78 x 82 x 94)
  Bookshelf    real size, turned 90 deg  180 x 33 x 180 (listed 180 x 32 x 185)
  Wardrobe     notice - the model doesn't match its listed size
  Stool        notice - the model is 83 cm tall, listed as 74 cm


PHASE 2 - RELIABLE FLOOR PLACEMENT
----------------------------------
  - Each surface the camera hits is classed as floor / raised (table, bed) /
    wall / other, from its direction and height.
  - Before the floor is set, the floor is the lowest level surface at least
    60 cm below the phone. The first tap fixes it ("Set floor here").
  - From then on the floor is exactly height 0 in the room: floor pieces can't
    float or sink, and a tap on a table or wall is refused with a hint.
  - The room is held by a WebXR anchor where the phone supports anchors, so
    ARCore's corrections move the whole room together and the gaps between
    pieces stay true.
  - If tracking is lost: placing and dragging stop, and the hint says to move
    slowly in good light.
  - Placement by product type (from its name or category):
      floor     - sofas, chairs, tables, beds, wardrobes, floor lamps...
      wall      - mirrors, paintings, wall shelves, clocks...
      tabletop  - table lamps, vases, planters... (on a table or the floor)
  - "Start over" clears everything, for when the floor was set on the wrong
    surface.


PHASE 3 + 4 - SEVERAL PIECES IN ONE ROOM, AND EDITING THEM
-----------------------------------------------------------
  - One AR session, one scene, one shared room frame for every piece.
  - Each model file is downloaded once and reused for copies and colours.
  - Up to 12 pieces at once (for phone memory).
  - The product drawer lists the catalogue, with each product's size and
    where it goes.
  - Select by tapping; move by dragging (along the floor, along a table top,
    or along the wall for wall pieces); turn by twisting or the 15 deg
    buttons (wall pieces always face out from their wall).
  - Move (pick up and put down somewhere else), Duplicate (placed beside the
    original), Delete, and colour swatches per piece.


PHASE 5 - OVERLAP AND WALKING SPACE (src/lib/room.ts)
------------------------------------------------------
  - Each piece is treated as a box: its floor footprint plus its height.
  - Overlap: footprints overlap by more than 1 cm AND the heights meet. A
    wall TV above a cabinet, or a lamp on a table, is not an overlap.
  - Tight: a gap between 15 cm and the walking space needed:
        60 cm normally,
        35 cm when one piece is a companion piece (coffee/side table,
        ottoman, lamp...).
    Gaps under 15 cm count as pushed together on purpose.
  - Checks run on real-world positions, not on the screen, so pieces that only
    look overlapped because of perspective are not flagged.
  - Boxes are approximate for curved or L-shaped pieces, so the wording is
    "may overlap".


PHASE 6 - ROOM MEASUREMENT
--------------------------
  - Corners: aim at each corner where the floor meets the walls, tap Add
    corner, then Finish room (3 or more corners; any shape).
  - Known size: type width x depth, mark one corner, then mark a point along
    the wall the width runs along. The room is built towards where you stand.
  - Once the floor is set, aiming follows the floor plane itself, so corners
    ARCore hasn't mapped yet can still be reached.
  - Shows the room's width x depth, area and each wall's length. Pieces
    crossing a wall are flagged red; the selected piece shows its distance to
    the nearest wall.
  - Ruler: two taps on the floor gives the distance in cm.
  - Not done: automatic wall detection or a 3D room scan. ARCore on Android
    phones has no room mesh, so measuring stays guided or typed.


FIT CHECK (src/lib/fit.ts, src/features/experience/FitCheck.tsx)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  - Shown on the store product page (under Dimensions) and on the QR /view
    page.
  - Compares the real product size with the space entered. It never resizes.
  - The space is remembered on the phone (localStorage) and offered in AR as
    "Your space".
  - Made-to-measure is not built (no shop sells it yet).

HOW TO TEST
===========
Setup
  1. npm run dev:tunnel (instead of dev:all). It starts the tunnel, the API and
     the site, and prints the new https://….trycloudflare.com address with a
     QR code. No .env editing needed.
  2. On an ARCore-supported Android phone, scan that QR code (or type the
     address) in Chrome and go to a product.
  3. The button must say "View in your room". If it says "View in AR" you are
     on plain http and in Google Scene Viewer, which has none of these features.
  4. Tap "View in your room". Allow the camera.

A. Real size (most important; needs a tape measure)
  - Tape 2.00 m along the floor. Place the Mahal sofa with one end on the
    start mark, turned so its width runs along the tape. Its far end should
    land about 212 cm from the start (aim for within about 3-4 cm).
  - Check from 1 m, 2 m and 3 m away.
  - The selected sofa's card should read about 212 x 91 x 75 cm and "real size".
  - Ruler check: measure the 2.00 m tape with Room & measure -> Ruler. If the
    ruler is off by the same amount as the sofa, the error is the phone's
    tracking, not the model.
  - Open the wardrobe or stool: an amber notice should say the model doesn't
    match its listed size. It still places.
  - Pinch should not resize anything.

B. Floor
  - Point at a table first: hint "looks raised", and Set floor is disabled.
  - Point at the floor: "Set floor here" -> the sofa sits on the floor. Look
    from low down: no gap under the legs, not sunk in.
  - With the floor set, aim at a sofa seat or table: placing a floor piece
    should be refused with a hint.
  - Cover the camera or move very fast: the "Tracking lost" hint appears and
    dragging stops. Uncover it: pieces should come back where they were.

C. Several pieces
  - Place sofa -> + Add furniture -> lounge chair -> place -> Duplicate it ->
    add a table.
  - Move one chair: the sofa and the other chair must not move.
  - Walk around the room for a minute and come back: every piece should still
    be on its tape mark (note how many cm it moved).
  - Change a chair's colour: only that chair changes.
  - Delete a piece; Move a piece to another spot.
  - Try to add a 13th piece: a message says the limit is 12.

D. Overlap and walking space
  - Drag the chair into the sofa: both outlines turn red; the card says
    "May overlap".
  - Leave about 40 cm between two chairs: amber, "too narrow to walk through".
  - Leave about 40 cm between the sofa and a coffee table: should be OK (35 cm
    rule for companion pieces).
  - Push two chairs fully together (under 15 cm): no warning.
  - Seen from an angle where they only look overlapped on screen: no warning.

E. Room
  - Room & measure -> Measure room by its corners -> tap the 4 corners ->
    Finish room. Compare width / depth / walls with a tape or laser measure.
    Write down the error.
  - Room & measure -> enter the real size -> mark a corner -> mark along the
    wall. The outline should sit on the real walls.
  - Drag a piece through a wall line: red, "Goes past the room's walls".
  - The selected piece shows its distance to the nearest wall.

F. Fit check (any browser, no AR needed)
  - Mahal sofa product page -> Will it fit? -> width 200, depth 90:
    "It doesn't fit. 12 cm too wide."
  - Width 220, depth 100: "It fits." with the spare cm shown.
  - Open AR -> + Add furniture -> Your space shows the same numbers -> Place my
    space -> a blue box. Place the sofa inside it.

G. Phone performance
  - Place 8-10 pieces and use AR for about 10 minutes: note whether it stays
    smooth, and whether the phone gets hot or the page reloads.
  - Note how long the sofa takes to load (the file is 13 MB; compressing it is
    recommended).
  - Repeat on one cheaper and one newer phone.

Write down the numbers (sofa error in cm, movement after walking around, room
measurement error) for each phone. Those numbers are what shows the sizes are
right, not just that the demo looks right.


FIXES AFTER THE FIRST PHONE TEST
--------------------------------
Reported: AR sometimes hangs; after placing one piece, the next one doesn't
appear.
  1. Taps were counted twice. Chrome also sends every tap on the AR screen to
     the AR session as a "select", which placed pieces too. Taps on buttons
     could therefore place or move a piece unexpectedly. Now cancelled
     (beforexrselect) in ArScene.tsx.
  2. Floor check too strict. A spot counted as floor only within 6 cm of the
     floor locked on the first tap. ARCore's floor estimate moves more than
     that, so later pieces were refused (amber ring, Place disabled). Now
     15 cm.
  3. No fallback over unmapped floor. Where ARCore hasn't mapped the floor yet,
     floor pieces now aim at the floor already found. A real table or wall
     under the ring still wins.
  4. Freezing. Any error inside the per-frame loop stopped the AR picture. The
     loop now logs the error and keeps drawing.
  5. Shadow map reduced from 2048 to 1024 (less GPU work per frame).
  6. After placing, the selected piece's card now has "+ Add another piece".
     Before, you had to tap Done to see "+ Add furniture".

Still heavy: Sofa.glb is 13 MB with 282,000 triangles and two 2048x2048
textures; every other model is about 1,000 triangles. Over the tunnel it
downloads slowly, and two sofas mean over a million triangles per frame. It
should be compressed / simplified (target under 5 MB).

KNOWN LIMITS
------------
  - iOS / non-WebXR phones keep the old single-product viewer (model-viewer),
    at the file's own scale.
  - The facing of a side-on model is a guess. Turn it if it faces the wrong way.
  - A wall piece's outline may show red if the measured room line doesn't
    exactly match the wall ARCore detected.
  - Wall detection on plain painted walls, and floor detection on glossy
    tiles or in low light, can be slow or fail. Move slowly and add light.
  - Placement type (floor / wall / table) is guessed from the product name and
    category. A database field would be more reliable later.
